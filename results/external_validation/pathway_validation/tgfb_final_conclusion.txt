ATLAS TGF-beta final evidence synthesis
========================================================================
Positive 16-gene module supported across datasets: True
Negative 7-gene module supported across datasets: True
Hallmark TGF-beta NES direction consistent across datasets: False

Defensible interpretation:
Trastuzumab resistance is associated with reproducible TGF-beta pathway remodeling, not a universally monotonic increase in the entire Hallmark TGF-beta pathway.
A 16-gene positive TGF-beta module is strongly activated in discovery and GSE237606 and trends upward in GSE121105, while a distinct 7-gene module is repressed across datasets.
Therefore, report pathway-level context dependence alongside reproducible submodule-level dysregulation.
